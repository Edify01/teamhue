Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

The short version TeamHue stores a conversation identifier and a color. It never reads, stores, or transmits the content of your messages. All data lives in a database your team controls.

What TeamHue stores

When you assign a color to a conversation, TeamHue saves:

  • A conversation identifier — an Instagram thread ID, a phone number, or an email address
  • A hex color (for example #6366f1)
  • An optional display label — the contact's name or handle, so lists stay readable
  • An optional owner and a short note you type yourself
  • Your account details — email address, display name, and team membership

What TeamHue never stores

  • The content of any message, email, call, or text
  • Attachments, photos, or files
  • Contact lists, browsing history, or activity on any other website
  • Passwords or credentials for Instagram, Google, or any other service

Why the permissions are needed

TeamHue requests access to Instagram, Google Voice, Gmail and Outlook because it must read the page structure to find conversation rows and apply colors to them. This access is used only to locate rows and style them. The extension never reads message bodies, and it never sends page content anywhere.

Where your data lives

TeamHue has no server of its own. Your team's data is stored in a Supabase database controlled by whoever set up your team. Data travels directly from your browser to that database over an encrypted connection. Access is enforced by row-level security, so only members of your team can read or change your team's colors.

What's stored on your computer

TeamHue keeps a local copy of your session and your team's color assignments in Chrome's extension storage. This makes the extension fast and lets it work briefly offline. It is removed when you sign out or uninstall.

Third parties

TeamHue contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. The only network requests it makes are to your own Supabase project.

Deleting your data

Remove individual colors from the extension's settings page at any time. Use Leave workspace to remove yourself from a team. To erase everything, the team owner can delete the workspace from the Supabase dashboard, which permanently removes all associated members and color assignments.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
